# Fennel and the Big Peach Shy
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 6 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming
Steps: Wave. Say. Share.

S1  | Clover the bunny turned five today, | and Fennel was invited to come and play!
S2  | He wrapped up a gift in a bow of blue. | “A party!” said Mama. “I’ll go with you.”
S3  | Balloons! And music! And cake on a plate! | So many faces! Fennel stopped at the gate.
S4  | A fuzzy peach cloud floated down, soft and slow. | It wrapped around Fennel from head to toe.
S5  | His voice went tiny. His cheeks felt hot. | He hid behind Mama and stayed on the spot.
S6  | Mama knelt down. “Shy is okay. | Lots of us feel shy, every day.”
S7  | “First, WAVE. Just lift your paw up high. | A wave is a way to say a shy hi.”
S8  | “Next, SAY one small hello. | ‘Hi, I’m Fennel.’ Say it soft and slow.”
S9  | “Last, SHARE. Have something to give. | A toy or a snack helps a friendship live.”
S10  | Fennel waved a wave, and it wasn’t so bad. | “Hi, I’m Fennel,” he said, feeling glad.
S11  | He shared his gift. Clover jumped with glee. | “Come play with me!” she said. “Come play with me!”
S12  | The peach cloud floated off like a feather. | Wave. Say. Share. Make friends together.
