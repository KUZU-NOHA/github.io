# Fennel and the Big Teal Lonely
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 9 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming
Steps: Look. Hello. Invite.

S1  | Fennel went to the park one day, | ready to swing and slide and play.
S2  | But Clover was gone to see her gran. | And everyone else had a friend or a plan.
S3  | Ducks played tag. Squirrels shared a ball. | Fennel stood still. He played with no one at all.
S4  | A big teal bubble rose up from the ground. | It wrapped him in quiet, without a sound.
S5  | Everything sounded far, far away. | He sat on a bench. He had nobody to play.
S6  | Mama came over. “Lonely is when | we’d love a friend, and we want one again.”
S7  | “First, LOOK around. Who is nearby? | Someone is there, with a kind little eye.”
S8  | “Next, say HELLO, with a smile that’s warm. | A hello can melt a lonely storm.”
S9  | “Last, INVITE. Ask someone to play. | ‘Want to swing with me?’ That’s all you say.”
S10  | Fennel looked. A hedgehog, small and shy, | was drawing in the dirt with a stick nearby.
S11  | “Hello!” said Fennel. “Want to play?” | “Yes!” said Pip. “I hoped you’d say!”
S12  | The teal bubble floated away, soft as a sigh. | Look. Hello. Invite. And a friend is nearby.
