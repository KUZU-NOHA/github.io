# Fennel and the Big Orange Scared
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 5 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming, social-emotional (fear of thunderstorms)
Text lives in build_book.py (STORY). Steps: SNUGGLE, COUNT (flash to boom), SHINE a small light.
Fear is shown as a fizzy orange spark that grows with each flash and shrinks to a tiny firefly-like glow.
