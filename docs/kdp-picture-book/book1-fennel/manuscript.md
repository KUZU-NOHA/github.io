# Fennel and the Big Red Mad
Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming, social-emotional (anger regulation)

Pages 1-2: title page, copyright. Pages 3-26: story (12 spreads). Pages 27-28: calm-down steps. Pages 29-30: note for grown-ups. 31-32: thanks / blank.

## Story (one block per spread: left page / right page)

S1  | Fennel Bear woke up with the sun, | ready for a day of fun.
S2  | He built a tower, tall and grand, | the tallest tower in all the land.
S3  | Then CRASH! It tumbled, block by block. | Fennel froze in total shock.
S4  | Grrrr went his tummy. His paws squeezed tight. | His cheeks puffed up with all his might.
S5  | "I'M MAD!" roared Fennel. "SO MAD!" he said. | The mad grew big like a red balloon, and it might go BOOM quite soon!
S6  | Mama Bear knelt down, soft and slow. | "Big mads are hard. I know, I know."
S7  | "Breathe in slow, smell a flower sweet. | Breathe out slow, blow the candle neat."
S8  | "Stomp your feet, one-two, one-two! | Shake the mad out, through and through!"
S9  | "Now tell me, Fennel, what is wrong?" | "My tower fell... it took so long."
S10 | The balloon got smaller, smaller still, | until it fit on his windowsill.
S11 | "Let's build it back together, then." | And Fennel smiled. And smiled again.
S12 | Now when big mads come knocking, you see, | Fennel knows just what to do: Breathe. Stomp. Tell. And he's calm as can be.

## Back matter
Fennel's 3 Calm-Down Steps: 1 BREATHE (flower, candle)  2 STOMP and shake  3 TELL someone what is wrong.
Note for grown-ups: name the feeling, stay calm, practice the three steps when everyone is calm, praise effort.
