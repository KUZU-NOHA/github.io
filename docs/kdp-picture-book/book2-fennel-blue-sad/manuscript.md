# Fennel and the Big Blue Sad
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 2 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming, social-emotional (sadness)

Pages 1-3 front matter, 4-27 story (12 spreads), 28-29 "Fennel's 3 Gentle Steps", 30 note for grown-ups, 31 thanks, 32 blank.

S1  | Fennel Bear had a kite so blue, | it zoomed and zipped the whole day through.
S2  | He ran up the hill with the wind in his face, | and the kite climbed high into sky-blue space.
S3  | Then WHOOSH! A gust, and the string slipped free. | Away flew the kite, past the tall oak tree.
S4  | Fennel stood still, as still as a stone, | with a heavy, achy feeling, all alone.
S5  | A tear rolled down, then two, then three, | till a big blue puddle grew round his knees.
S6  | “I’m SAD,” sniffled Fennel. “So sad,” he said. | “The Big Blue Sad is inside my head.”
S7  | Mama Bear sat down right by his side. | “Sad is okay,” she said. “No need to hide.”
S8  | “First, NOTICE it. Where does sad sit? | In your tummy? Your chest? Just point to it.”
S9  | “Next, CRY. Let the rain come out. | Tears help wash the sad about.”
S10 | “Last, CUDDLE, snuggly, slow and tight.” | Mama’s big hug made the puddle shrink right.
S11 | The puddle got smaller, and smaller, and small, | and the sun peeked out, warm over all.
S12 | Now when big blues come, Fennel knows what to do: | Notice. Cry. Cuddle. The sun comes back through.

Back matter: Fennel's 3 Gentle Steps: 1 NOTICE where sad sits. 2 CRY, let the rain come out. 3 CUDDLE, a hug helps.
