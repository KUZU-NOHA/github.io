# Fennel's Feelings — Books 6-10 plan

Pattern: "Fennel and the Big [Color] [Feeling]". Each book = 12 rhyming spreads, one visual metaphor for the feeling, three kind steps, a note for grown-ups. Same cast (Fennel, Mama Bear; Papa Bear and Baby Poppy from book 4; Clover the bunny from book 3).

| # | Title | Feeling | Metaphor | 3 steps | New cast |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Fennel and the Big Peach Shy | shyness (friend's birthday party) | a soft, fuzzy peach cloud-blanket that muffles his voice | Wave. Say. Share. | Clover (bunny), party animals |
| 7 | Fennel and the Big Yellow Excited | excitement (night before the fair) | bouncy yellow sparks / a springy sun that makes him bounce | Wiggle. Breathe. Wait. | Papa Bear |
| 8 | Fennel and the Big Purple Frustrated | frustration (learning to tie shoes) | a coiled purple spring that gets tighter | Pause. Try. Ask. | Papa Bear |
| 9 | Fennel and the Big Teal Lonely | loneliness (park, nobody to play with) | a quiet teal bubble that makes everything echo | Look. Hello. Invite. | Pip (hedgehog) |
| 10 | Fennel and the Big Rainbow of Feelings | all feelings (rainy-day finale) | a jar holding all nine feeling-marks, ending in a rainbow | recap of all steps | Mama, Papa, Poppy cameos |
