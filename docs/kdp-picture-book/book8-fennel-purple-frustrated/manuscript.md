# Fennel and the Big Purple Frustrated
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 8 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming
Steps: Pause. Try. Ask.

S1  | Fennel wanted to tie his shoes | all by himself — there was nothing to lose!
S2  | He made a loop. He made a twirl. | The laces just flopped in a floppy swirl.
S3  | He pulled and he pulled. It came out a mess. | He tried once more. And once more. No success.
S4  | A purple coil popped out of his chest. | It wound itself tight. It would not rest.
S5  | Tighter and tighter, the coil went round. | “I can’t! I can’t!” he said with a frown.
S6  | He tossed down his shoe. It flew through the air. | Papa Bear came and sat on the stair.
S7  | “Frustrated is hard,” said Papa. “I know. | Big things take time. We can take it slow.”
S8  | “First, PAUSE. Put your shoe down and shake. | Shake out your paws, for goodness’ sake!”
S9  | “Next, TRY a new way. Make bunny ears two. | Cross them and tuck them. See what you can do.”
S10  | “Last, ASK for a hand. It’s not giving in. | A helping paw is a place to begin.”
S11  | Loop, swoop, pull — and the knot held tight! | The purple coil faded from sight.
S12  | Now when the purple gets tight and tough, | Fennel knows: Pause. Try. Ask. That’s enough!
