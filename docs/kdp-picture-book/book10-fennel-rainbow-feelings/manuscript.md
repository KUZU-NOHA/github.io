# Fennel and the Big Rainbow of Feelings
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 10 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming
Steps: Feel It. Name It. Share It.

S1  | Rain tapped the window. Rain tapped the door. | Fennel stayed in. What was in store?
S2  | Mama set nine jars down in a row, | each with a small light, glowing aglow.
S3  | Red for his Mad, a balloon that grew. | Blue for his Sad, a puddle, too.
S4  | Gray for his Worry, a tangle of thread. | Green for his Jealous, a vine in his head.
S5  | Orange for Scared, a spark in the night. | Peach for his Shy, a cloud, soft and light.
S6  | Yellow for Excited, all bounce and zing. | Purple for Frustrated, a tight little spring.
S7  | Teal for his Lonely, a bubble so round. | Nine feelings, each one, he had found.
S8  | “Feelings come, and feelings go. | Each one helps us learn and grow.”
S9  | “Feel it. Name it. Share it, too. | That’s all you need. That’s all you do.”
S10  | He told Papa Bear, “I felt scared last night.” | “Me too,” said Papa, and they hugged tight.
S11  | The rain slowed down. The clouds rolled away. | The sun peeked out on a bright new day.
S12  | A rainbow stretched out, from jar to jar. | Every feeling is part of who we are.
