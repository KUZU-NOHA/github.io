# Fennel and the Big Yellow Excited
Series: Fennel's Feelings, book 7 | Ages 3-6 | 32 pages | 8.5x8.5 in | rhyming
Steps: Wiggle. Breathe. Wait.

S1  | Tomorrow was the Fair! Hooray, hooray! | Fennel could not wait for the big, big day!
S2  | He counted his coins. He brushed his hair. | He was so excited he danced on the stair!
S3  | At bedtime, a yellow spark went POP! | It bounced on his pillow, hop, hop, hop!
S4  | More sparks came bouncing round and round, | bouncing off the walls with a boing-y sound.
S5  | The Big Yellow Excited filled the room. | Fennel bounced on the bed. Zoom, zoom, ZOOM!
S6  | His toys went flying. His pillow too. | “I can’t lie down!” he said. “What do I do?”
S7  | Papa Bear came and sat on the bed. | “Excited is big,” the kind bear said.
S8  | “First, WIGGLE it out! Shake your paws, shake your knees!” | Fennel wiggled and giggled and shook like the breeze.
S9  | “Next, BREATHE. Puff your tummy up big and round, | then let the air out with a soft, whooshy sound.”
S10  | “Last, WAIT. Make a countdown. Cross off the night. | Tomorrow is coming. Tonight, hold on tight.”
S11  | The sparks bounced slower, and slower, then slept. | Fennel cuddled in close, and a sweet dream crept.
S12  | Next day at the Fair, with its lights shining bright, | Fennel wiggled and breathed and he waited just right.
